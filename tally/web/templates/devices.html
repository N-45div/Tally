Desk devices

A desk device announces invoices as they are held and answers questions about the queue. It can put an invoice on hold and nothing more: it cannot approve, reject or release anything. It speaks only words from Tally's own records (vendor names and phone numbers on file, amounts), never words printed on an invoice. Every question and every hold is in the ledger.
