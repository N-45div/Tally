Limits of the lab

{{ icon.info() }}

The attack files are Tally's own, not collected from real fraud. The naive agent is one model family, given the text layer only. The numbers are small: 24 files, three tries each for the naive agent. The benchmark's invoices are synthetic too, though in 50 layouts Tally was never shown, and its labels are only as good as the dataset's.
